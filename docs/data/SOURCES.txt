Bundled benchmark instances

These files are unmodified copies of public benchmark instances, bundled so that the Solver works without third-party hosts.

* `cvrp/X-n*.vrp`: the 100 instances of the X set of Uchoa et al. (2017), "New benchmark instances for the capacitated vehicle routing problem", EJOR 257(3):845-858, copied from the instance folder of <https://github.com/vidalt/HGS-CVRP>. The full set is at <http://vrp.galgos.inf.puc-rio.br/> (CVRPLIB).
* `cvrp/CMT1..CMT14.vrp`: instances of Christofides, Mingozzi and Toth (1979), "The vehicle routing problem", in Christofides, Mingozzi, Toth and Sandi (eds.), Combinatorial Optimization, Wiley, 315-338, copied from the same folder of <https://github.com/vidalt/HGS-CVRP>. Distances are not rounded; CMT6-10, 13 and 14 have a route-duration limit (`DISTANCE`) and a service time (`SERVICE_TIME`).
* `cvrp/Golden_1..Golden_20.vrp`: instances of Golden, Wasil, Kelly and Chao (1998), "The impact of metaheuristics on solving the vehicle routing problem: algorithms, problem sets, and computational results", in Crainic and Laporte (eds.), Fleet Management and Logistics, Kluwer, 33-56, copied from the same folder. Distances are not rounded; Golden_1-8 have a route-duration limit.
* `mdvrp/p01..p23`, `mdvrp/pr01..pr10`: multi-depot instances of Cordeau, Gendreau and Laporte (1997), "A tabu search heuristic for periodic and multi-depot vehicle routing problems", Networks 30(2):105-119, copied from <https://github.com/STOM-Group/MDVRP-Instances>.

Best-known values of the X, CMT and Golden instances are those listed by CVRPLIB (checked on 2026-11-22).

The original URL of every file is the `source` field in `../assets/instances.js`. All credit for the instances goes to their authors.
